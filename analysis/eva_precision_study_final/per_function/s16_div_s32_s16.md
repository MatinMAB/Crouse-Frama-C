# s16_div_s32_s16

## Experiment Summary

Baseline reports 25 canonical Eva alarms and High Precision reports 20. Five integer-overflow entries are removed; 20 remain and none are new. Both runs analyze 2 of 26 functions and reach 51 of 51 normalized statements. Parse diagnostics and the target final-state text are identical.

## Baseline Result

The five selected Baseline markers occur in two guarded remainder computations at `div_Ccode.c:274` and `div_Ccode.c:297`. They comprise four subtraction-bound checks and one quotient upper-bound check.

## High Precision Result

No canonical counterpart for these five markers occurs in the High Precision log. This absence is not explained by reduced reachability: both runs report full 51/51 statement reachability.

## Removed Alarm Inventory

### Alarm 1 — RA-001

#### Location

`src_analysis/sources/div_Ccode.c:274`

#### Category

Integer overflow, lower bound of `s32Temp1 - ((s32Temp1 / denominator) * denominator)`.

#### Source Expression and Context

`s32Temp1` is copied from the `s32` numerator. The expression is in the `denominator != 0` branch and the `numerator != INT32_MIN` sub-branch. Division, multiplication, and subtraction are performed as signed 32-bit operations.

#### Baseline Evidence

The Baseline log emits the lower-bound marker at line 274.

#### High Precision Evidence

The marker is absent with identical parse diagnostics, coverage, and target final-state text.

#### Candidate Precision Mechanism

`NONLINEAR_SUBDIVISION`: stronger treatment of the quotient-product dependency is the most plausible primary aid.

#### Confidence

MEDIUM.

#### Direct Evidence

The marker transition, copy, guards, and unchanged reachability are directly observable.

#### Technical Inference

The combined relational and nonlinear profile plausibly establishes a tighter mathematical remainder interval; no single option is isolated.

### Alarm 2 — RA-002

#### Location and Category

`div_Ccode.c:274`, integer-overflow upper bound of the same remainder subtraction.

#### Source Context

The operands and guards are the same as RA-001.

#### Baseline and High Precision Evidence

Baseline emits the upper-bound marker; High Precision does not, while statement reachability stays 51/51.

#### Candidate Precision Mechanism and Confidence

`NONLINEAR_SUBDIVISION`, MEDIUM. The inference is that dependency loss in division followed by multiplication is reduced by the combined profile.

### Alarm 3 — RA-003

#### Location and Category

`div_Ccode.c:274`, integer overflow for `s32Temp1 / denominator <= INT32_MAX`.

#### Source Context

The path has `denominator != 0` and `numerator != INT32_MIN`. The exceptional signed quotient `INT32_MIN / -1` therefore requires a numerator value excluded by the active branch.

#### Baseline and High Precision Evidence

Baseline emits the quotient-bound marker; High Precision does not. The same source statement is reached in both runs.

#### Candidate Precision Mechanism and Confidence

`BRANCH_PARTITIONING`, MEDIUM. Stronger branch history likely retains the numerator exclusion at the division; the exact contributing option remains inseparable.

### Alarm 4 — RA-004

#### Location and Category

`div_Ccode.c:297`, integer-overflow lower bound of the remainder subtraction.

#### Source Context

This is the `numerator == INT32_MIN` path. Before line 297, the source explicitly excludes `denominator == -1`, and the outer branch already excludes zero.

#### Baseline and High Precision Evidence

Baseline emits the lower-bound marker; High Precision does not; coverage is unchanged.

#### Candidate Precision Mechanism and Confidence

`BRANCH_PARTITIONING`, MEDIUM. The nested conjunction is the central direct structure; its preservation is a technical inference.

### Alarm 5 — RA-005

#### Location and Category

`div_Ccode.c:297`, integer-overflow upper bound of the same subtraction.

#### Baseline and High Precision Evidence

Baseline emits the upper-bound marker and High Precision does not. Parse and final-state comparisons remain identical.

#### Candidate Precision Mechanism and Confidence

`BRANCH_PARTITIONING`, MEDIUM, for the same nested-guard reason as RA-004.

## Function-level Observation

This function provides the largest count reduction: five. Three removals are most directly associated with branch-sensitive exclusion of exceptional signed-division cases, while two are most consistent with improved nonlinear remainder bounds.

## Limitations

All High Precision features were enabled together. The labels are primary explanations, not per-option proof traces, and no runtime classification is made.
